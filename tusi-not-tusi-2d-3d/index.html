Tusi and Not-Tusi

Tusi and Not-Tusi illusions show how coherent global forms can emerge from simple, identical local motions. In Tusi displays, points move back and forth along fixed lines; in Not-Tusi displays, they move along fixed circles. Change only the temporal phase relationships between the points, and the same local trajectories produce circles, clovers, stars, and other global patterns.
